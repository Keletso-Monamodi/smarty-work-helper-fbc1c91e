My workmate App.

AI-powered office tools built with lovable.dev
1. Problem = I have notice that people at work waste time on drafting emails, reading long meeting notes and also looking for information by searching.

Solution = I built workmate AI assistant that automates Email generator, Summarizes and chatbot for searching. These will reduce time wasting.

workmate assist workers to save time with 3 tools or tabs: 
Email generator = writing email in 20seconds
Meeting summarizer = pasting notes and get action items and decisions
Chatbot = Ask questions and IT assistance.

2. tools used
- lovable.dev = built the app by using AI prompts.
- ChatGPT = for text and AI features 
- PowerPoint for presentation 

3. Challenges and Solutions 
-Challenge 1: AI is not always 100% sometimes makes mistakes by writing wrong spelling,  date and write what you didn't ask.
  Solution: You need to make AI understand what you want and the correct date to avoid mistakes and double check before sending your outcomes.
- Challenge 2: Meeting summaries were too long.  
  Solution: Added at least 8 bullet points to avoid taking time to read the whole notes. And to force concise results.
- Challenge 3: Lovable.ai needed understandable instructions to build App.
  Solution: Wrote step-by-step prompt for Lovabl to Create page with 3 tabs: Email, Summarize and Chatbot 

4. Improvement. 

Before creating AI assistant, workers was taking 10+ minutes to write email, taking 20+ minutes writing meeting notes. Spending 30+ minutes searching something on internet. 

After building this app workers will be able to do everything in less than 3 minutes on each task they want to do.
